# Ryan — Part 4: Context to intent

“Before someone knows what to search for, useful context is scattered across what they read. Needle connects opted-in observations into a possible need, shows its evidence and uncertainty, and asks the person before finding a product.”

The flow is **opt in → measure → review → explicitly connect with AI → confirm or correct → find an option**.

Cursor dwell is a weak attention signal, not proof of buying intent. The distinctive demonstration connects source meanings into an inspectable hypothesis with a human correction step. It does not claim dwell tracking itself is new or that existing recommenders never use it.

The demo measures real cursor duration across two prepared source identities on one local origin. It is not yet arbitrary cross-site instrumentation. AI can assess bounded observations beyond the prepared topics; a separately labelled rule-based demo works without an AI key.

Existing Save inspiration, Find products, screenshot identification, clarification, and voice remain team workflows. Part 4 adds a context-to-need layer. It does not place orders or connect seller analytics to live browsing.
